<text>
<idea>
  <design_movement>Aerospace Industrialism / Cockpit UI</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **High Contrast & Legibility**: Critical data must be readable at a glance, mimicking flight instruments.
    2. **Precision & Grid**: Layouts are strictly aligned, evoking engineering blueprints and technical manuals.
    3. **Functional Density**: Information is dense but organized, prioritizing utility over decorative whitespace.
    4. **Status Indication**: Clear, color-coded indicators for system status (On Track, Delayed, Critical).
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **Dark Mode Default**: To reduce eye strain during long planning sessions, similar to modern glass cockpits.
    **Palette**: Deep slate greys and blacks for backgrounds. Neon cyan, amber, and magenta for data points (HUD style).
    **Intent**: Evoke the feeling of controlling a complex system with precision.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **Dashboard-Centric**: A fixed sidebar for controls (like a side-stick console) and a top bar for global status (annunciator panel). The main view is the "Flight Path" (Gantt).
    **Modular Panels**: Collapsible panels with visible borders and "rivet" details or technical corner accents.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Monospaced Numbers**: Use tabular lining figures for all dates and durations.
    2. **Technical Borders**: Thin, 1px borders with corner brackets or ticks.
    3. **HUD Overlays**: Semi-transparent overlays for modals or context menus.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    **Tactile Feedback**: Buttons have a distinct "pressed" state. Toggles look like physical switches.
    **Snap & Lock**: Dragging items feels magnetic, snapping precisely to grid lines (time slots).
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    **Instant & Linear**: Transitions are fast (100-150ms) with linear easing. No bounce or spring.
    **Scanlines**: Subtle scanning effect on data load.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    **Headings**: 'JetBrains Mono' or 'Share Tech Mono' (uppercase, tracked out).
    **Body**: 'Inter' or 'Roboto' for readability, but with tight leading.
  </typography_system>
</idea>
</text>

<idea>
  <design_movement>Swiss International / Airport Wayfinding</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **Clarity & Neutrality**: The interface is a vessel for content. No distractions.
    2. **Strong Hierarchy**: Size and weight dictate importance.
    3. **Universal Symbolism**: Use standard aviation iconography (ISO style).
    4. **Asymmetric Balance**: Dynamic layouts that guide the eye naturally.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **High-Key / Light**: White and light grey backgrounds, mimicking the clean, airy feel of a modern terminal.
    **Palette**: International Orange (safety), Airport Blue (information), and Signal Yellow (warning).
    **Intent**: Create a sense of calm organization amidst complex logistics.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **Horizontal Flow**: Emphasize the timeline nature. Wide, horizontal bands for phases.
    **Typography-Driven**: Large, bold headings separate sections. Minimal borders; whitespace defines structure.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Big Typography**: Massive phase numbers (01, 02, 03) as background elements or section headers.
    2. **Directional Arrows**: Heavy use of arrows to indicate flow and dependencies.
    3. **Frosted Glass**: Subtle blur on sticky headers, like airport signage.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    **Smooth Flow**: Scrolling is smooth. Hover states are subtle shifts in brightness.
    **Contextual Reveal**: Details appear only when needed (progressive disclosure).
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    **Ease-Out**: Smooth, decelerating movements (300ms).
    **Slide & Fade**: Elements slide in from the direction of flow (left to right).
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    **Headings**: 'Helvetica Now' or 'Arial' (Bold, tight tracking).
    **Body**: 'Helvetica Now' or 'Inter' (Regular).
  </typography_system>
</idea>

<idea>
  <design_movement>Neo-Brutalism / Cargo Logistics</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **Raw & Unapologetic**: Exposed structures, high contrast, bold outlines.
    2. **Object-Oriented**: Tasks are treated as physical "crates" or "containers".
    3. **High Visibility**: Everything is clearly defined with thick borders.
    4. **Utilitarian**: Form follows function strictly.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **Stark Contrast**: Off-white or cardboard beige backgrounds.
    **Palette**: Pure black for text/borders. Vibrant, unmixed colors (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow) for task categories.
    **Intent**: To feel like a robust, heavy-duty tool that can handle the "heavy lifting" of project management.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **Card-Based**: Everything is a card with a hard shadow (no blur).
    **Masonry/Grid**: Strict adherence to a visible grid system.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Hard Shadows**: 4px solid black offset shadows.
    2. **Thick Borders**: 2px-3px solid black borders on everything.
    3. **Marquee**: Scrolling text for urgent alerts.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    **Physicality**: Buttons depress fully (shadow disappears).
    **Drag & Drop**: Items feel heavy; maybe a slight tilt when dragged.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    **Step / Frame-by-Frame**: Jerky, mechanical movements.
    **No Fade**: Elements pop in/out instantly.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    **Headings**: 'Space Grotesk' or 'Chivo' (Black weight).
    **Body**: 'Space Mono' or 'Courier Prime' (for data).
  </typography_system>
</idea>
</text>
